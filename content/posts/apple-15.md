AirPods 4 introduced noise cancellation on an open-ear earbud and it was better than nothing, which was the honest review. AirPods 5 are Apple's second attempt, and the claim is a specific one: up to 50 percent more external noise removed than AirPods 4 with ANC. Here is the full announcement and why the cheaper of the two models is the more interesting product.

![AirPods 5](/media/2026/09/b66255aff659cf53.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

## What changed inside

The driver and enclosure are a new **multiport acoustic architecture**, the design language from AirPods Pro 3 applied to a bud that does not seal the ear. Next-generation Adaptive EQ tunes the output to the ear it finds itself in, which for an open design that sits differently on everyone is the part that decides whether the bass is there or not. Personalised Spatial Audio gets more headroom from the same change.

**Active Noise Cancellation** is where the effort went. Apple attributes the 50 percent improvement to better computational audio rather than a new seal, because there is no seal; the microphones and the algorithms are doing all of it. **Transparency** is described as more natural for voices and nearby sounds. **Adaptive Audio** blends the two automatically, and **Conversation Awareness** drops the volume when you start talking.

## Battery and case

- Up to **5 hours** of listening with ANC on, an hour more than AirPods 4.
- Up to **22 hours** total with the case.
- The Wireless Charging Case (on the $149 model only) charges from USB-C, any Qi pad, or an Apple Watch charger.

![The Wireless Charging Case](/media/2026/09/3ada83a043b3311c.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

## Controls and durability

The $149 model adds a force sensor on the stem with **volume swipes**, which Apple says is a first for the open-ear form factor. Both models improve dust, sweat and water resistance over AirPods 4; Apple did not give an IP rating in the release. Siri AI works hands-free, with head gestures for yes and no on incoming calls and messages. **Live Translation** is on board: press both stems, or ask, and the other person's language comes through in yours, which needs an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone.

## Two models, one decision

| | AirPods 5 | AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $129 | $149 |
| ANC and Transparency | yes | yes |
| Case charging | USB-C | USB-C, Qi, Apple Watch charger |
| Volume swipe on stem | no | yes |
| Battery | 5 h + case | longer total, per Apple |

For $20 the wireless case and volume control are an easy yes if you already own a Qi pad or a MagSafe charger. If you do not, the base model is the best value Apple has offered in an earbud with real noise cancellation.

## Where they sit in the lineup

AirPods Pro 3 remain the choice for anyone who wants a sealed fit, the heart-rate sensor and the hearing-aid features. AirPods 5 are for people who dislike silicone tips, which is more people than the Pro sales suggest, and who have put up with hearing the whole train to avoid them. AirPods 4 without ANC presumably continue as the entry model.

## Dates

Pre-orders opened today. In stores Friday, September 18, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and the new watches.

## My take

The interesting number is not the 50 percent, which nobody can verify from a keynote; it is that the ANC model costs $129. That puts noise cancellation, spatial audio, Adaptive Audio and Live Translation at the price where AirPods used to have none of them. If the isolation is genuinely closer to the Pros than to AirPods 4, this becomes the default earbud for most people, and I expect to find out on a Delhi Metro platform on the 18th.
