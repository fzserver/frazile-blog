God of War turned twenty this year, and Santa Monica Studio celebrated with more announcements in one year than the series usually gets in five. There is a new game with a new lead, a 2D prequel you can already play, a remake of where it all started, and a TV series that had to find a new Kratos halfway through filming. Here is where each one stands.

## God of War Laufey: February 16, 2027

Announced at the June 2 State of Play and dated at San Diego Comic-Con in July, **God of War Laufey** is out on **PS5 on February 16, 2027**.

The lead is **Laufey**, known to Kratos and Atreus as **Faye**, the wife whose ashes started the 2018 game. It begins with her funeral. Faye wakes somewhere she did not expect: the **Everywhen**, an afterlife shared by gods from every mythology, where they fight over territory and power. She finds that the plans she made to protect Kratos and Atreus are coming apart, and she fights her way through the Everywhen to save them.

The story runs **alongside God of War (2018) and Ragnarök**, not after them, so expect scenes from those games seen from the other side. It is the first mainline game with Kratos out of the lead role. **Deborah Ann Woll** plays Laufey, **Christopher Judge** is back as Kratos, and Jack Quaid and Perlina Lau join the cast. **Ariel Lawrence** directs, with Cory Barlog as head of creative.

The last two games only ever hinted that Faye was a warrior who could match Kratos. This is the game that has to show it, and her combat style is the biggest thing to watch as more of the game is shown. February 2027 is also getting crowded, so expect a few of its neighbours to move.

## Sons of Sparta: the one you can play now

Released on **February 12, 2026**, **God of War Sons of Sparta** is a **2D side-scrolling Metroidvania** from Mega Cat Studios with Santa Monica. It follows a 13-year-old Kratos and his brother **Deimos** training at the Agoge in Sparta, years before the first game. It is a smaller, lower-priced game, and a love letter to the Greek era. If Laufey's February date feels far off, it is the easy way back into the series.

## The original trilogy, remade

At the February State of Play, Santa Monica confirmed that it is **remaking God of War, God of War II and God of War III**. The key detail is "**early development**": this was an anniversary announcement, not a release window. It is also why the studio is known to be working on several projects at once, including a new franchise set in the God of War universe.

For anyone who started with the 2018 game, the Greek trilogy is the half of Kratos's story they have only heard about. A modern remake is the best way to fill that in, whenever it arrives.

## The Amazon series and its new Kratos

Prime Video's live-action series has had a rough run. **Ronald D. Moore** (Battlestar Galactica, For All Mankind) took over as showrunner in October 2024, Amazon ordered **two seasons**, and filming started in **Vancouver on February 27, 2026**, with both seasons shot back to back into 2027.

In June, **Ryan Hurst**, cast as Kratos, **tore his bicep** during a stunt. The recovery was longer than the schedule could allow, so the production recast. **Dave Bautista** was confirmed as the new Kratos on **August 20**. Four finished episodes are being reshot with him. Callum Vinson plays Atreus and Teresa Palmer plays Sif.

There is no premiere date. With filming now running into 2027 and reshoots added, late 2027 at the earliest is a reasonable guess.

## What to play while you wait

Replay **God of War (2018)** and **Ragnarök**, including the free *Valhalla* roguelite mode. Laufey fills in the edges of those two games, and it will land harder with them fresh in your mind. Both are on PS5 and PC. Play *Sons of Sparta* if you want something new this year. Then watch for more of Faye in action as February gets closer.
