Apple's "Surprise and shine" event ran this morning at Apple Park, and for once the tagline was earned: the surprise is a phone that folds. This post is the map. Each product gets a paragraph and a link to its own write-up, followed by the dates that matter if you plan to buy any of it.

*Image: Apple*

## iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max

The Pro phones got the changes that photographers have wanted for years: a variable aperture on the 48MP main camera, with six laser-cut blades stepping between ƒ/1.48, ƒ/1.8, ƒ/2.8 and ƒ/4, plus manual Pro controls in the Camera app. Underneath is the A20 Pro on a 2 nm process, a vapor chamber three times larger than last year's, Apple's own C2 modem and N1 wireless chip, and battery life of up to 36 and 45 hours of video. The Dynamic Island shrank and now shows three Live Activities. Four colours, 256 GB to 2 TB, $1,199 and $1,299. [Full post.](/p/iphone-18-pro)

There is no plain iPhone 18 this year. Apple has split the cycle: the standard models come in spring.

## iPhone Duo

The foldable. A 7.6-inch inner display and a 5.4-inch outer one, grade 5 titanium polished to a mirror, a hinge with more than a hundred parts, and a version of iOS 27 laid out for two screens with real side-by-side multitasking. Same A20 Pro as the Pro phones, dual batteries rated for 31 hours of video on the inner screen. Two colours, star white and night sky, from $1,999. Pre-orders open October 16 and it ships October 23 with iOS 27.1. [Full post.](/p/iphone-duo)

## Apple Watch Series 12

New optical and electrical heart sensors that Apple calls the Health Sensing System: heart rate every five seconds all day, heart rate variability 24 times more often than before, and a daily Readiness score from 0 to 10. The S11 chip adds on-device audio features, including a Live Rewind that shows the last 15 seconds of a conversation as text. Ceramic Shield 2 on the aluminium models, a new ceramic case in pearl white and night blue, and workout battery up 25 percent. From $399, pre-orders open now, in stores September 18. [Full post.](/p/apple-watch-series-12)

## Apple Watch Ultra 4

The same sensor and chip in the adventure watch, with the battery numbers that model exists for: 50 hours normal use, 84 in Low Power Mode, 25 hours of full-GPS workout and 45 in the new Max Extended mode built for 100-mile races. Natural and black titanium, from $799, September 18. [Full post.](/p/apple-watch-ultra-4)

## AirPods 5

Open-ear Active Noise Cancellation that removes up to 50 percent more noise than AirPods 4, a new acoustic architecture borrowed from AirPods Pro 3, five hours of listening with ANC on, and better dust and water resistance. $129, or $149 with a wireless charging case and on-stem volume swipes. In stores September 18. [Full post.](/p/airpods-5)

## iOS 27, watchOS 27 and the new Siri

The software ships on Monday, September 14, for every iPhone back to the iPhone 11. The headline is Siri AI, a rebuilt assistant with its own app, personal context, and on-screen awareness, in beta and English-only at launch with five more languages in October. A redesigned Health app with Insights and Longevity tabs comes later in the year. [Full post.](/p/ios-27-siri-health-app)

## What did not happen

No new iPad, no new Mac. The M6 Mac mini and the M5 Ultra Mac Studio were announced quietly on August 25 and ship September 22, so they did not need stage time. The iPhone 17 Pro models are gone from the store, and the remaining iPhone 16, 17, 17e and Air all went up by $100.

## My take

The Duo is the story, and $1,999 with 256 GB is lower than most people guessed. But the product most people should actually buy is the one with the least drama: the Series 12 sensor upgrade is the first Apple Watch change in years that alters what the data is worth, and it costs the same $399 as before. The Pro phones are a strong year for anyone who takes photos and a skippable one for everyone else.
