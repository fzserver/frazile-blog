Most Apple Watch years are a new colour and a chip you cannot feel. Series 12 is the other kind. The heart sensor is new, front and back, and the cadence of measurement changes from "a few times an hour" to "every five seconds, all day". That reshapes what the Health app can honestly say about you. This is the full announcement, sensor first.

![Apple Watch Series 12](/media/2026/09/8f075c2118e534b3.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

## The Health Sensing System

New optical and electrical heart sensors with larger, more efficient green LEDs. Apple's numbers:

- Heart rate measured **every 5 seconds** throughout the day, not only during workouts.
- Heart rate variability up to **24 times more often** than before, roughly every five minutes.
- Two HRV figures instead of one: **Recovery HRV**, for day-to-day stress and recovery, and **overall HRV** for the broader cardiovascular picture.
- Apple calls it the most accurate heart rate sensing in a wearable, citing its own study of more than a thousand participants run this July and August.

Vitals gains a daytime view alongside the overnight one, and overnight now includes recovery HRV against your personal baseline. The practical effect: a bad night, an illness coming on or a week of overtraining should show up as a shift in the daytime numbers before it appears in the morning summary.

## Readiness

The feature people will actually look at. **Readiness** combines recent activity, training load, vitals and sleep into a 0 to 10 score each morning, with one of four verdicts: Recover, Pace Yourself, Ready, or Go For It. It updates through the day as new data arrives. The algorithm was built from the Apple Heart and Movement Study with exercise scientists and physicians, which is Apple's way of saying it is not a Garmin-style black box copied from a competitor. The pedometer was rewritten too, with new machine learning models and a Steps complication that updates live.

## S11 and the audio features

The S11 is Apple's most powerful watch chip, and the reason it needed to be is a set of features that process sound on the wrist:

- **Live Rewind**: double-press the Digital Crown and the last 15 seconds of a conversation appears as text. For the moment you missed a name, or an address.
- **Siri Recap**: a summary of a conversation with a title and key points, not a transcript.
- **Sound Recognition**: sirens, alarms, doorbells, a crying baby, without an iPhone nearby.
- **Shazam** on the Music Recognition widget, faster.

Audio is handled in a Secure Exclave, a hardware-isolated part of the chip, and deleted immediately; no recording is stored, and Live Rewind plays an audible chime so the people around you know. Live Rewind and Siri Recap ship in beta later this year. All four need a Series 12 or Ultra 4.

## Battery, glass, materials

- 24 hours of everyday battery, unchanged, but **10 hours of outdoor workout**, up 25 percent, and 15 minutes of charging now gives 12 hours.
- **Ceramic Shield 2** on the aluminium models, 60 percent tougher than the Ion-X glass it replaces.
- Aluminium in dark bronze, black, light gold and space gray. Titanium in a new radiant gold and natural. And a new **ceramic** case in pearl white and night blue, with matching ceramic Sport Band closures. 42 mm and 46 mm.

![Pearl white ceramic with a sand Sport Band](/media/2026/09/5d4a22fc9a14f0e6.jpg)

Bands: new Sport Band colours (olive, burgundy, wildflower blue, pale succulent), plaid Sport Loops printed by inkjet, a Milanese Loop in radiant gold and dark bronze, and a Modern Buckle in a new woven material with gold hardware for the first time. Hermès gets radiant gold titanium and a Rocabar Club band.

## watchOS 27

Siri AI arrives on the wrist with its own app and a Siri Modular face. The app grid becomes dynamic with five Siri-suggested apps, the Smart Stack gets a one-handed tap gesture, Cycle Tracking adds perimenopause and menopause support, and Workout Buddy runs without an iPhone and now speaks Spanish. It ships September 14 for Series 9 and later, SE 3 and Ultra 2 and later, paired to an iPhone 11 or newer on iOS 27.

The redesigned Health app with its Insights and Longevity tabs, Health Age and in-app lab testing is a bigger topic; [it has its own post](/p/ios-27-siri-health-app).

## Price and dates

From **$399**, the same as Series 11. Pre-orders opened today; in stores Friday, September 18, in more than 60 countries including India. New subscribers get three months of Fitness+ and Apple Music.

## Should you upgrade

From a Series 9, 10 or 11: this is the first year I would say yes for the health data alone, because the sampling rate is what makes the trend features trustworthy rather than decorative. From an SE or anything older: it is not close. From an Ultra 2 or 3: read the [Ultra 4 post](/p/apple-watch-ultra-4) first, since the same sensor and chip arrived there with the battery you actually bought an Ultra for.
