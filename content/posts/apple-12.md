Apple waited until the seventh year of foldable phones to make one, and the iPhone Duo is what that patience bought: a book-style foldable with an inner display Apple says is 50 percent larger than the 18 Pro Max, an outer display close to a normal phone's, and an operating system that was reworked for the form rather than stretched over it. It ships October 23 from $1,999. Here is everything Apple said and what I make of it.

![iPhone Duo open, showing the 7.6-inch inner display next to the 5.4-inch outer one](/media/2026/09/72e2f184dac9fe8c.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

## The hardware

**Displays.** Inner: 7.6-inch Super Retina XDR. Outer: 5.4-inch, which Apple says gives 90 percent of the screen area of an iPhone 18 Pro, so the phone is usable closed and not just a viewfinder for the big screen. Both have ProMotion, always-on, and 3000 nits peak. The inner panel has a nano-texture finish that cuts glare and, in Apple's words, minimises the visibility of the crease. Note the wording: minimises, not removes.

**Body.** Grade 5 titanium polished to a mirror, a 3D-printed hinge cover in 100 percent recycled titanium, Ceramic Shield on the back and Ceramic Shield 2 on the outer display. IP68 to six metres for 30 minutes, which no other foldable I know of matches. Open, it is the thinnest iPhone Apple has made. The hinge has more than a hundred components; the cover layer over the folding panel is a custom polymer with 40 percent more stiffness than what Apple tested against.

**Silicon.** The same A20 Pro as the 18 Pro, with its own vapor chamber, the N1 wireless chip (Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, Thread) and the C2 modem. The Duo is eSIM-only everywhere, including India, which will be a first for a lot of people here.

**Batteries.** One in each half, managed as a single pack: up to 31 hours of video on the inner display, 44 on the outer, and 24 hours of mixed use split between them. 50 percent in about 20 minutes with a 60 W cable, 30 minutes on MagSafe or Qi2.

## Cameras

Two on the back: a 48MP Fusion main with an integrated 2x optical-quality telephoto and a 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide, the same pair as the 18 Pro minus the dedicated telephoto. A Center Stage front camera in the outer display and a FaceTime camera hidden under the inner one. 4K120 Dolby Vision video, cinematic effects after capture, and the new texture and grain controls in Photographic Styles.

The folding-specific tricks are the fun part. **Duo Preview** shows the subject a live preview on the outer display while you shoot with the main cameras, so selfies use the good lenses. **Smart Take** watches the scene and fires when people are posing. **Kid Cue** plays Peanuts animations on the outer screen to get a child to look at the camera, which is either the most Apple feature ever or the most useful one, and probably both. **Duo FaceTime** lets someone on the other side of the phone join a call from the outer display.

## iOS 27 on a folding phone

This is what I was watching for, because the hardware in foldables has been fine for years and the software has not. Apple moved the lock screen controls, the Dock and app navigation to the sides when open, so vertical space goes to content. The Home Screen shows two pages side by side with a vertically scrolling Today View on the left. The status bar becomes a small circle; the Dynamic Island runs vertically along the edge. Content reorients when the phone is turned and switches displays when you flip it.

**Split View** brings two apps side by side to iPhone for the first time, with quick app swapping, two windows of the same app, and saved app pairs. Siri AI gets its own app and can hold a conversation in one pane while you read in the other. StandBy works on either display without a charger. Netflix, Zoom, Slack and Detail were shown already adapted; Apple's own apps are done.

![Safari and the Siri app side by side](/media/2026/09/f4a6ce357a86eed6.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

The Duo ships with iOS 27.1, not 27.0, which tells you the foldable-specific work landed on a later branch than the September release.

## Price, colours, dates

- Star white and night sky. 256 GB, 512 GB, 1 TB, 2 TB.
- $1,999, or $83.29 a month over 24 months in the US.
- Pre-orders Friday, October 16 at 5 a.m. Pacific. In stores Friday, October 23 in more than 70 countries, India included, with 28 more on October 30.
- A $79 case in sand or navy blue, and a $129 folio with a kickstand in navy blue or taupe.

## What I think

Two thousand dollars for 256 GB undercuts the guesses and matches the top Android foldables at launch. The IP68 rating, the outer display size and the multitasking model are the three things that make me think this is the first foldable I would carry rather than admire. The open questions are the ones a press release cannot answer: how visible the crease is under a lamp, how the hinge sounds in a year, and whether the eSIM-only choice causes trouble for Indian carriers on day one. I will find out in October.
