The software section of an Apple event is usually a date, and this year it was also a product: Siri AI, the rebuilt assistant Apple has been previewing since June, reaches iPhones next Monday. This post covers the release schedule, what the new Siri actually does, the health features that go with the new watches, and the compatibility and language footnotes that decide whether any of it reaches your phone.

## Dates and compatibility

- **iOS 27, iPadOS 27, watchOS 27**: free updates on **Monday, September 14**.
- iOS 27 runs on everything that ran iOS 26: iPhone 11 and later, and iPhone SE 2nd generation and later.
- watchOS 27 needs a Series 9 or later, SE 3, or Ultra 2 or later, paired with an iPhone 11 or newer on iOS 27.
- The iPhone Duo ships with iOS 27.1 in October, which is where the two-screen layout lives.
- The redesigned Health app arrives **later this year**, in US English first.

## Siri AI

An entirely new Siri built on Apple Intelligence and Apple's Foundation Models, on device and through Private Cloud Compute, shipping as a **beta**. It gets its own app with a synced conversation history, and can be called from the Dynamic Island. The capabilities Apple listed:

- **Personal context**: it can draw on your messages, mail and photos to answer "when does the flight land" without you finding the mail.
- **On-screen awareness**: act on whatever is on the display, such as adding an address from a message to a contact.
- General knowledge questions, in conversation.
- A **Siri mode in the Camera app** on the 18 Pro and Duo, for asking about what the camera sees.
- **Write with Siri**: describe what you need and it drafts it.

![The Siri app on iPhone Duo](/media/2026/09/624730e1751ef94a.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

Languages: English on September 14, then French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish in October. Not available in the EU or China at launch, and not for users under 13. The most demanding Siri features need the newest phones: iPhone Duo, 18 Pro and Pro Max, iPhone Air and 17 Pro. Older Apple Intelligence phones get the rest.

Other iOS 27 items from the release: Spatial Reframing, Extend and an improved Clean Up in Photos, a photorealistic mode for Image Playground, Safari's **Notify Me** for watching a page for a restock or price drop, a transparency slider for Liquid Glass, better parental controls, and **iPhone Handoff**, which lets two iPhones share one number on supporting carriers.

## The redesigned Health app

This is the piece that turns the new watch sensors into something you can read. It comes later in 2026 on Apple Intelligence-capable iPhones and iPads.

![The redesigned Health app with Apple Watch Series 12](/media/2026/09/2f127bb457b40d5b.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

**Insights** is a new tab of daily summaries across heart, sleep, readiness, fitness, vitals and cycle tracking, with a For You section of articles and recommendations tied to your own data.

**Longevity** looks at the long run across seven areas: heart health, sleep, mental wellbeing, movement, metabolic health, hearing and nutrition. Inside it, **Health Age** compares your metrics, VO2 max, resting heart rate, sleep, HRV and optionally lab values like A1c and LDL, against your chronological age. It needs an Apple Watch.

![Health Age in the Longevity tab](/media/2026/09/026f79a805cac1bc.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

**Movement evaluations** use the iPhone camera and the watch to assess flexibility, strength, balance and movement mechanics at home, with the video processed on device and never stored. **Labs** lets US users order a panel of more than 50 biomarkers for $119 at about 2,000 Quest Diagnostics locations and get the results back into the app, with explainers that overlay your numbers.

**Cycle Tracking** adds perimenopause and menopause logging, alerts for post-menopausal bleeding, and, for users over 40, a notification when logged patterns suggest perimenopause.

![Perimenopause support in Cycle Tracking](/media/2026/09/c2a121eb24abfafd.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

Also: Workout Buddy runs on the watch without a phone nearby and adds Spanish, AirPods Pro 3 can send heart rate to GymKit treadmills and bikes, and treadmill distance is calculated with new models from the first step.

## The footnotes that matter

Every Apple Intelligence feature carries the same three qualifiers: device, language, region. India gets English on day one and is on the list for more Apple Intelligence languages, but the Health app's Insights and Longevity content starts in US English and the lab ordering is US-only. Readiness, the new vitals views and the audio features are tied to the [Series 12](/p/apple-watch-series-12) and [Ultra 4](/p/apple-watch-ultra-4) hardware, not to the software update.

## What to do on Monday

Update. iOS 27 is the stable release of a beta that has been running since June, and the performance work on app launch, Photos, Camera and Wi-Fi handoff applies to every supported phone. Turn on Siri AI if you are in English, treat it as the beta it is labelled as, and give the personal-context features a week before judging them; they get better as the index fills in. The Health app is the one to wait for.
