The Ultra line has always been the Apple Watch for people who count hours, and Ultra 4 is mostly a battery and sensor story. That is fine. It is exactly the story the last two Ultras needed. Here is everything Apple announced for it, and how it differs from the Series 12 launched beside it.

![Apple Watch Ultra 4](/media/2026/09/37b5379784dc9d6b.jpg)
*Image: Apple*

## Battery

The numbers, from Apple:

| Mode | Ultra 4 |
|---|---|
| Normal use | up to 50 hours |
| Low Power Mode | up to 84 hours |
| Extended Workout (full GPS + heart rate) | up to 25 hours, 25% more than Ultra 3 |
| Max Extended Workout (1-second GPS) | up to 45 hours |
| 15 minutes of charging | adds 18 hours |

The new **Max Extended Workout** mode is the one built for a 100-mile race: one-second GPS sampling for 45 hours, which is longer than most people's cut-off times. The 25-hour full-fidelity figure covers a long day in the mountains with some margin. And "more than two days" of normal use means you can leave the charger at home for a weekend and track sleep both nights.

## The Health Sensing System

Ultra 4 gets the same new optical and electrical heart sensors as Series 12, so all of this applies:

- Heart rate every 5 seconds, all day.
- HRV up to 24 times more often, split into Recovery HRV and overall HRV.
- A daily **Readiness** score from 0 to 10 with a Recover, Pace Yourself, Ready or Go For It verdict, built on recent activity, training load, vitals and sleep.
- Daytime vitals alongside the overnight view, and a rewritten pedometer with a live Steps complication.

For an Ultra owner the Readiness score is the feature that closes the gap with Garmin's Body Battery and Whoop's recovery, and it is built from Apple's own Heart and Movement Study data rather than licensed from anyone.

## GPS and connectivity

Apple claims the most accurate on-device GPS of any sports watch, tested in dense urban environments where multipath reflections normally wreck a track. Satellite messaging and Emergency SOS remain, with two years included from activation. Cellular is standard on the Ultra.

## S11 and audio intelligence

The S11 chip powers the same on-device sound features as Series 12: **Live Rewind** (double-press the crown, get the last 15 seconds of a conversation as text), **Siri Recap** summaries, **Sound Recognition** for sirens, alarms and doorbells without an iPhone, and faster Shazam. Audio is processed inside a Secure Exclave and deleted; nothing is recorded. Live Rewind and Siri Recap come in beta later this year.

## Design, bands, Hermès

Natural and black titanium, 100 percent recycled in the 3D-printed case. The display stays the large, bright one. New bands for the season: Trail Loop in dark umber, sand and burgundy; Alpine Loop in desert, dark olive and burgundy; and an Ocean Band in a new translucent silicone (gray, kelp, black). Hermès Ultra 4 gets a Grand H titanium bracelet with a built-in wetsuit extension and a "Fathom and Wonder" ocean face with four depth views.

## watchOS 27

Siri AI with its own app, a dynamic app grid, one-handed Smart Stack navigation, Workout Buddy without an iPhone and now in Spanish, perimenopause support in Cycle Tracking. Arrives September 14. The redesigned Health app, Health Age and in-app lab tests are covered in the [software post](/p/ios-27-siri-health-app).

## Price and dates

From **$799**, unchanged. Pre-orders opened today; in stores Friday, September 18 in more than 60 countries including India. $24.99 a month over 24 months in the US.

## Ultra 4 or Series 12

Same sensor, same chip, same software. The Ultra buys you roughly double the battery, the 45-hour GPS mode, the bigger and brighter display, satellite, and titanium by default, for $400 more. If your longest activity is a two-hour ride, the [Series 12](/p/apple-watch-series-12) in titanium is the better watch. If you regularly outrun a Series battery, or you want to track sleep across a multi-day trip without thinking about a charger, this is the one, and for Ultra 2 owners specifically the sensor plus the workout battery is enough to justify the change.
